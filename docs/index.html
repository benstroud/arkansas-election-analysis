How to read the numbers

Democratic share
Democratic votes as a percentage of Democratic plus Republican votes. Third-party votes are left out.
Latest race
The 2026 result where both parties ran. Otherwise the 2024 result.
Swing
The 2026 share minus the 2024 share in the same district. Positive means a shift toward Democrats.
Republican-held
Below 50 percent in the latest race. This is not confirmed incumbency.
2028 Senate class
The 18 seats contested in 2024, including District 1. Inferred from the 2024 results. Not yet checked against the official roster.
Not a forecast
The list ranks seats by how close the last contest was. It does not model 2028 turnout, candidates, or the national environment.

Caveats

  • The 2026 results are unofficial and may change after certification.
  • 2024 was a presidential year and 2026 a midterm. Turnout differs, so swings mix changes in opinion with changes in who voted.
  • Incumbency, retirements, open seats, and 2028 candidates are not included. Seats without a Democrat on the 2026 ballot are ranked but cannot flip without a candidate.

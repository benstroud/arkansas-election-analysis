# Arkansas Election Analysis

Working project to identify the state legislative seats most likely to flip to Democrats in 2028 (Arkansas House and Senate). This README is the running log of steps taken, data sources, and their provenance.

## Layout

```
data/raw/            Source files as obtained. Do not edit; record provenance below.
  sos/               Arkansas Secretary of State 2026 general and judicial runoff results (CSV)
  tiger2022_sldl/    Census TIGER/Line 2022 Arkansas state House districts
  tiger2022_sldu/    Census TIGER/Line 2022 Arkansas state Senate districts
  tiger2024_sldl/    Census TIGER/Line 2024 Arkansas state House districts
  tiger2024_sldu/    Census TIGER/Line 2024 Arkansas state Senate districts
  medsl2024/         MIT Election Data + Science Lab 2024 state precinct returns (README, codebook; the 1.2 GB national CSV is not tracked in git, see .gitignore)
data/derived/        Filtered and derived files, regenerated by scripts
  ar_2024_state_legislative.csv  Arkansas 2024 State House and Senate rows from the MEDSL file (20,884 rows)
  ar_2024_vs_2026_by_district.csv  Comparison table: one row per House district and Senate seat, with 2024 and 2026 D two-party share, swing, Senate class, and candidate flags (136 rows)
scripts/             Analysis and download scripts
  review_sos_2026.py            Validates the SOS 2026 CSV and tabulates House/Senate results by district
  extract_ar_2024_legislative.py Streams the MEDSL 2024 file and writes Arkansas legislative rows
  summarize_ar_2024.py          Tabulates 2024 Arkansas legislative results by district
  compare_2024_2026.py          Joins 2024 and 2026 results by district and writes the comparison table
  rank_2028_targets.py          Ranks Republican-held 2028-class seats by latest Democratic two-party share
  check_ar_2024_presidential.py Checks Arkansas 2024 presidential totals against the MEDSL README; tests the PROVISIONAL mode
data/derived/ar_2028_target_ranking.csv  Ranked list of 44 Republican-held seats in the 2028 class (38 House, 6 Senate)
README.md            This file
```

## Steps log

- **2026-11-11.** Discussed approach for identifying likely 2028 Democratic pickups in the Arkansas legislature. Recommended precinct-level results joined to district boundaries, with partisan lean per district.
- **2026-11-11.** Checked data sources. Confirmed the Harvard Dataverse API, the Census TIGER directories, and the Arkansas SOS site are reachable. Confirmed MEDSL 2024 covers Arkansas and that TIGER has 100 House and 35 Senate features for both 2022 and 2024.
- **2026-11-11 14:43 CST.** Downloaded four Arkansas TIGER/Line legislative district shapefile zips (2022 and 2024, House and Senate) into `data/raw/`.
- **2026-11-11 14:44 CST.** SOS 2026 results CSV present in `~/Downloads` (user-supplied).
- **2026-11-11.** Wrote `scripts/review_sos_2026.py`. The first version mishandled contest name variants, candidate party tokens, and precinct IDs that repeat across counties. Revised to aggregate from county rows, normalize district numbers, and normalize party tokens (`R` and `REP` both map to Republican).
- **2026-11-11.** Validation of the SOS CSV: 100% of legislative county rows report. Candidate totals reconcile with `Total Votes` for all 258 county contest pairs. Absentee + early + election-day votes equal candidate votes for every legislative row.
- **2026-11-11 14:50 CST.** Copied SOS CSV into `data/raw/sos/` under a shell-safe filename. Verified the copy's SHA-256 matches the original.
- **2026-11-11 15:13–15:15 CST.** Downloaded the MEDSL 2024 state precinct dataset (national CSV, README, codebook) into `data/raw/medsl2024/`. Confirmed the CSV size matches the Dataverse metadata. Recorded the license as CC0 1.0.
- **2026-11-11 15:15 CST.** Wrote `scripts/extract_ar_2024_legislative.py` and filtered Arkansas State House and Senate rows to `data/derived/ar_2024_state_legislative.csv` (20,884 rows; 13,883 House, 7,001 Senate).
- **2026-11-11.** Wrote `scripts/summarize_ar_2024.py`. Summarized 2024 results by district: 100 House districts and 18 Senate districts.
- **2026-11-11.** Found that Senate District 1 has races in both the 2024 file (R Ben Gilmore vs. Libertarian Asher Williams, not flagged special) and the 2026 file (D Tomeka Butler vs. R Harrell Wilson). Left unresolved. See open items.
- **2026-11-11.** Wrote `scripts/compare_2024_2026.py` and built `data/derived/ar_2024_vs_2026_by_district.csv`. It covers all 101 House districts (100 plus the 2026 District 8 special) and all 35 Senate seats. Two-party share is D/(D+R), and swing is 2026 minus 2024, computed only where both years are contested by a Democrat and a Republican.
- **2026-11-11.** Findings from the comparison. House: 25 districts contested in both years, all 25 moved toward Democrats. Mean swing +9.2 points, median +9.7, range +5.0 to +14.6. Republican-held 2026 districts contested in both years: 81 (39.8 → 49.5), 14 (34.8 → 48.2), 34 (38.0 → 48.0), 50 (36.0 → 43.4), 36 (32.1 → 41.1), 53 (26.6 → 39.0), 69 (28.7 → 38.5), 51, 54, 25, 24. Senate: no seat was contested by both parties in both years. Senate District 1 had no Democrat in 2024 (Republican vs. Libertarian) and a Democrat in 2026, so it isn't counted in the both-years comparison. See open items. The 2028 Senate class (2024 cycle) has no 2026 result, so its only baseline is 2024.
- **2026-11-11.** Resolved Senate District 1. Senator Ben Gilmore (R) resigned effective June 30, 2026, to join the Attorney General's office, and the Secretary of State's office said a special election would fill the seat ([KARK](https://www.kark.com/news/politics/arkansas-sen-ben-gilmore-resigning-to-move-to-attorney-generals-office/)). Gilmore won the 2024 regular election ([Arkansas Advocate](https://arkansasadvocate.com/2024/11/06/republican-incumbents-sweep-seven-contested-arkansas-senate-races/)). The 2026 SD 1 race (Butler D vs. Wilson R) is therefore the special election, and the SOS file doesn't flag it. The script now treats it as special.
- **2026-11-11.** Senate class, working conclusion. The 2024 file has 18 regular Senate contests and the 2026 file has 17 regular contests plus the SD 1 special. This matches a report that 17 of 35 Senate seats were up in 2026 ([5NEWS](https://www.5newsonline.com/article/news/politics/elections/arkansas-elections-results-legislative-races-state-house-senate/527-47212601-0c68-40fd-990e-d721702e9d6d); the figure comes from a search summary, and I did not open the page). Conclusion: the 2028 class is the 18 seats contested in 2024, including District 1. The 2030 class is the 17 seats contested in 2026. The Ballotpedia pages I tried to fetch came back empty, so this is not yet confirmed against an official source.
- **2026-11-11.** Wrote `scripts/rank_2028_targets.py` and built `data/derived/ar_2028_target_ranking.csv`. Method: latest result is 2026 where contested, else 2024. Targets are seats whose latest result is a Republican win in the 2028 class. The House is entirely 2028, so all 100 seats are eligible. Seats where a Democrat was unopposed in 2026 are excluded (14 House seats). Result: 44 targets (38 House, 6 Senate). 58 2028-class seats had no contested result in either year and are not ranked.
- **2026-11-11.** Validated the 2024 aggregation (open item 4 of the next-steps list). The state file has no presidential rows, so I downloaded the MEDSL per-state Arkansas file (`2024-ar-precinct-general.tab`, 44 MB). Summing all modes, including PROVISIONAL, reproduces the MEDSL README's precinct totals exactly for Trump (759,132), Harris (396,824), Oliver, Stein, Sonski, and Wood. Excluding PROVISIONAL is off by 200–300 votes for the top two, so PROVISIONAL is included. The per-state file and the state file (our legislative source) agree exactly on all 20,884 Arkansas legislative rows, 181 candidate-district keys, and 1,622,986 total votes. Script: `scripts/check_ar_2024_presidential.py`.
- **2026-11-11.** Created this README.
- **2026-11-11.** Recorded the source URL for the SOS CSV (Tally ENR page, see Data sources table) from the user's screenshot of the download menu. Screenshot not copied into the project.
- **2026-11-11.** Recorded the direct download link for the "Full Election" CSV from the user and confirmed the download option. Added to the Data sources table.

### Headline results from the 2026 SOS file (unofficial, not certified)

- House: 101 contests. 55 with no Democratic candidate, 17 with no Republican candidate, 63 unopposed, 19 contested in the 40–60% two-party range.
- Senate: 18 contests (not the full 35). 10 with no Democratic candidate, 3 with no Republican candidate, 12 unopposed.
- Closest Republican-won House seats by Democratic two-party share: House 81 (49.5%), 14 (48.2%), 34 (48.0%), 50 (43.4%), 36 (41.1%).
- Closest Republican-won Senate seats: Senate 27 (46.4%), 16 (41.4%).
- Democratic-won seats by under two points: House 10 (50.8%), 32 (50.7%), 55 (51.5%).

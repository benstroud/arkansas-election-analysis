## Steps log

- **2026-11-11.** Discussed the approach for identifying likely 2028 Democratic pickups in the Arkansas legislature. Recommended precinct-level results joined to district boundaries, with partisan lean per district.
- **2026-11-11.** Checked data sources. Confirmed the Harvard Dataverse API, the Census TIGER directories, and the Arkansas SOS site are reachable. Confirmed MEDSL 2024 covers Arkansas and that TIGER has 100 House and 35 Senate features for both 2022 and 2024.
- **2026-11-11 14:43 CST.** Downloaded four Arkansas TIGER/Line legislative district shapefile zips (2022 and 2024, House and Senate) into `data/raw/`.
- **2026-11-11 14:44 CST.** SOS 2026 results CSV present in `~/Downloads` (user-supplied).
- **2026-11-11.** Wrote `scripts/review_sos_2026.py`. The first version mishandled contest name variants, candidate party tokens, and precinct IDs that repeat across counties. Revised to aggregate from county rows, normalize district numbers, and normalize party tokens (`R` and `REP` both map to Republican).
- **2026-11-11.** Validated the SOS CSV. 100% of legislative county rows report. Candidate totals reconcile with `Total Votes` for all 258 county contest pairs. Absentee, early, and election-day votes equal candidate votes for every legislative row.
- **2026-11-11 14:50 CST.** Copied the SOS CSV into `data/raw/sos/` under a shell-safe filename. Verified the copy's SHA-256 matches the original.
- **2026-11-11.** Recorded the source URL for the SOS CSV (Tally ENR page) from the user's screenshot of the download menu. Screenshot not copied into the project.
- **2026-11-11.** Recorded the direct download link for the "Full Election" CSV from the user and confirmed the download option.
- **2026-11-11 15:13–15:15 CST.** Downloaded the MEDSL 2024 state precinct dataset (national CSV, README, codebook) into `data/raw/medsl2024/`. Confirmed the CSV size matches the Dataverse metadata. Recorded the license as CC0 1.0.
- **2026-11-11 15:15 CST.** Wrote `scripts/extract_ar_2024_legislative.py` and filtered Arkansas State House and Senate rows to `data/derived/ar_2024_state_legislative.csv` (20,884 rows: 13,883 House, 7,001 Senate).
- **2026-11-11.** Wrote `scripts/summarize_ar_2024.py`. Summarized 2024 results by district: 100 House districts and 18 Senate districts.
- **2026-11-11.** Found that Senate District 1 had races in both the 2024 file (R Ben Gilmore vs. Libertarian Asher Williams) and the 2026 file (D Tomeka Butler vs. R Harrell Wilson).
- **2026-11-11.** Wrote `scripts/compare_2024_2026.py` and built `data/derived/ar_2024_vs_2026_by_district.csv`. Covers all 101 House districts (100 plus the 2026 District 8 special) and all 35 Senate seats.
- **2026-11-11.** Comparison findings. House: 25 districts contested in both years, all 25 moved toward Democrats. Mean swing +9.2 points, median +9.7, range +5.0 to +14.6. The 2028 Senate class has no 2026 result, so its only baseline is 2024.
- **2026-11-11.** Resolved Senate District 1. Senator Ben Gilmore (R) resigned effective June 30, 2026, to join the Attorney General's office, and the Secretary of State's office said a special election would fill the seat ([KARK](https://www.kark.com/news/politics/arkansas-sen-ben-gilmore-resigning-to-move-to-attorney-generals-office/)). Gilmore won the 2024 regular election ([Arkansas Advocate](https://arkansasadvocate.com/2024/11/06/republican-incumbents-sweep-seven-contested-arkansas-senate-races/)). The 2026 District 1 race is therefore the special election, and the SOS file doesn't flag it. The script now treats it as special.
- **2026-11-11.** Senate class, working conclusion. The 2024 file has 18 regular Senate contests and the 2026 file has 17 regular contests plus the District 1 special. This matches a report that 17 of 35 Senate seats were up in 2026 ([5NEWS](https://www.5newsonline.com/article/news/politics/elections/arkansas-elections-results-legislative-races-state-house-senate/527-47212601-0c68-40fd-990e-d721702e9d6d); the figure comes from a search summary, and the page was not opened). The Ballotpedia pages tried later came back empty, so this is not yet confirmed against an official source.
- **2026-11-11.** Wrote `scripts/rank_2028_targets.py` and built `data/derived/ar_2028_target_ranking.csv`. Excluded seats where a Democrat was unopposed in 2026 (14 House seats). Result: 44 targets (38 House, 6 Senate). 58 2028-class seats had no contested result in either year and are not ranked.
- **2026-11-11.** Validated the 2024 aggregation. The state file has no presidential rows, so I downloaded the MEDSL per-state Arkansas file. Summing all modes, including PROVISIONAL, reproduces the MEDSL README's precinct totals exactly for Trump (759,132), Harris (396,824), and the four minor candidates. Excluding PROVISIONAL is off by 212 and 278 votes for the top two. The per-state file and the state file agree exactly on all 20,884 Arkansas legislative rows, 181 candidate-district keys, and 1,622,986 total votes. Script: `scripts/check_ar_2024_presidential.py`.
